Privacy

Privacy.

What the mod and this site do with your data. In short: the mod sends nothing, and the site sets no cookies.

Who is responsible

Autobuild GUI and this site are run by Sebi. For anything about your data, message @aquaxs on Telegram.

The mod

It runs entirely in your Minecraft client and makes no network requests of its own: no telemetry, no update check, no server of ours.

It keeps two settings in config/autobuildgui.json on your computer; the key binding lives in Minecraft's own options.txt. Baritone and Litematica are separate mods with their own terms.

This website

Hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). To deliver the pages and protect them against abuse, Vercel processes connection data — IP address, time, requested file and browser. Legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Vercel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

No cookies, no tracking, no analytics. The fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Stored in your browser

Once you break a block in the background, the number of blocks you have mined is kept in your browser's localStorage under autobuildgui:mined, so the counter survives a reload. Nothing is stored before that.

The number never leaves your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes it.
